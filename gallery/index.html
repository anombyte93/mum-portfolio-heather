Full moon rises beside the illuminated Busselton Jetty pavilions, twin light sources — cold lunar silver and warm tungsten gold — writing parallel ribbons across still water.

Solitary figure wading at the shoreline beneath the silhouetted Busselton Jetty boat sheds, a moored hull and sailing mast held against a dusk gradient over silk water.

Pink chrysanthemum pom and yellow gerbera crowded against dark foliage, soft window light grazing the tightly packed petals.

Dusk at the travelling carnival: the Cliff Hanger's arm smears into colour while its lit signage holds firm above the fenced lawn.

A child settles into a neon-lit bumper car beneath a pavilion, a row of waiting vehicles glowing in formation behind her.

Father and child share a bumper car under carnival lights, neon reflections pooling across the polished arena floor.

Busselton Jetty at dusk, the pavilion and red tourist train glowing against a slate sky, their warm lights bleeding into glassy water.

Gravitron and ticket booth face off across a patch of dark midway, neon geometry holding the night while small figures drift between the rides.

Bumper cars idle under magenta and cyan neon, their tail lights bleeding into the polished floor as an operator in red waits at the rail.

Defocused neon signage dissolves into stacked cyan capsules and a glowing yellow orb, mirrored on wet pavement below a magenta awning.

Sheltie pauses mid-turn on a pale cushion, mane haloed by window light, tongue just visible against the dark guard-hairs of the ruff.

A weathered timber jetty strides out over glassy shallows, its pylons casting long shadows across submerged seagrass and pale sand.

Empty coastal cycle path cuts a red-ochre band between dune fencing and the deep blue of the Indian Ocean.

Weathered granite outcrop meets wind-sculpted coastal scrub above a pale, hazed bay, midday light flattening the textures of stone and leaf.
